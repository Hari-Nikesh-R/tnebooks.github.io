The Challenge .. Opportunity

1. Systemic Operational Flaw

Emergency tools forced teachers onto fragmented online tools without digitizing the end-to-end teaching workflow or catering to low-bandwidth environments.

2. Hardware Cost

Prohibitive procurement costs for enterprise-grade hardware in rural institutions.

3. Software Licensing Trap

High recurring per-seat licensing fees making proprietary EdTech completely unsustainable for government and rural schools.

Our Story (Why We Are Different)

Technology Assisted Teaching (TaT) and TNEBooks represent a grassroots, open-source educational ecosystem engineered specifically for the realities of Indian government and aided schools. Born from hands-on field trials in Ramanathapuram, our platform bridges the digital divide by treating quality education as an open public good.

Co-created by industry mentors (TNJUG), university engineering teams (Kongu Engineering College), and passionate school alumni, TNEBooks addresses real classroom constraints: zero software costs, offline-first local operation, simplified lesson planning, and self-guided revision modules.

"We do not merely deliver digital files; we empower teachers with automated tools and ignite student curiosity through community-driven open-source education."
